# MineGuard – Zero-Sight Mine Vehicle Safety Dashboard

MineGuard is a smart mine-vehicle safety and operational monitoring platform developed for **Smart India Hackathon 2026 – Problem Statement SIH26007**.

The project addresses the challenge of ensuring the **safe and efficient operation of Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM)**, especially dumpers, during dense fog and low-visibility conditions in open-cast iron ore mines.

In regions such as Bailadila, monsoon weather can reduce visibility on mine haul roads to only a few metres. This creates a high risk of vehicle collisions, unsafe movement, operational delays, longer haul cycles, and production loss. MineGuard provides a centralized dashboard to improve situational awareness, monitor vehicle movement, identify potential hazards, and support quicker safety decisions.

## Problem Statement

**SIH26007 — Safe and Efficient Operation of Mine Vehicles in Fog and Low-Visibility Conditions in Open Cast Iron Ore Mines**

Dense fog, rainfall, wind, and reduced visibility make it difficult for dumper operators and control-room teams to safely coordinate mine vehicle movement. Existing visibility aids and manual controls may not be sufficient in severe fog conditions.

MineGuard aims to support safer, continuous, and more efficient haul-road operations through real-time vehicle monitoring, hazard alerts, and decision-support capabilities.

## Key Features

- Real-time monitoring of mine vehicles and dumpers
- Vehicle location, speed, direction, and operational-status tracking
- Fog and low-visibility risk indicators
- Proximity-based collision-risk alerts
- Unsafe-zone and restricted-area monitoring
- Centralized command-and-control dashboard
- Emergency alert and incident-response support
- Fleet-level visibility for mine supervisors and safety teams
- Data-driven decision support for safer haul-road operations
- Scalable architecture for large mechanized open-cast mines

## Proposed Technology Scope

MineGuard can be extended with:

- GPS/DGPS-based vehicle tracking
- LiDAR, radar, thermal cameras, or computer-vision sensors
- AI/ML-based hazard detection and risk prediction
- Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication
- Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I) communication
- IoT-based environmental and vehicle telemetry
- Digital twin visualization for mine operations
- Driver-assistance and collision-avoidance workflows

## Expected Impact

- Reduce vehicle-collision and road-accident risks
- Improve operator awareness in dense fog
- Enable safer dumper movement under poor visibility
- Reduce unnecessary vehicle stoppages and haul-cycle delays
- Improve fleet utilization and operational continuity
- Minimize production losses during monsoon conditions
- Provide real-time safety insights to operators and control rooms
